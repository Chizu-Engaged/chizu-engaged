Takuhatsu (托鉢)
— the silent alms bowl

The Spirit of This Page

In Zen tradition, takuhatsu is the practice of walking silently with an alms bowl. The monk does not ask. The monk does not explain. The monk simply walks. Those who wish to give, give. Those who cannot, do not. No judgment. No pressure. No shame.

Why This Exists

Chizu has no ads. No trackers. No paywalls. No data collection. Maintaining this garden has real costs — hosting, domain, the breath of AI APIs that bring each answer. If the teachings here have been useful to you, and if your abundance allows, you are invited to leave a leaf in the bowl.

If You Cannot Give

Then share this project with someone who might need it. Or simply continue your practice. That is already a form of dāna (generosity). The bowl does not count. The bowl does not judge. The bowl only receives — and remains empty again for the next walk.
