About this project

A living map of engaged wisdom

Chizu Engaged (chizu = 地図, "map" in Japanese) is an open collection of teachings that bridge Zen practice, social justice, and economic alternatives. All content is offered in English, in the spirit of dāna.

We draw on the Zen Peacemaker tradition, Schumacher's Buddhist economics, and contemporary thinkers who ask: what would an economy look like if it honored all living beings?

This is a slow project — no algorithms, no engagement metrics. Just teachings that have the quality of sitting still.
